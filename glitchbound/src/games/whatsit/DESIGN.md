# Whatsit

A daily "twenty questions" with a line-up. A brown-paper parcel (the Whatsit) hides one thing from the
day's theme. You ask the theme's yes/no questions, then say "I know it", see a line-up of 12 things from
the theme and make one pick. No going back for more questions.

## The round

- **Theme**: one of 14 (instruments, sea creatures, dishes, space, toys, curious animals, kitchen tools,
  weather, sports gear, bugs, gadgets, plants, clothes, vehicles), shown from the start.
- **Questions**: 8–14 per theme, each with a clear yes/no for every one of the theme's 16 things. The bank
  test makes sure every thing in a theme has its own set of answers, so asking everything always singles
  the secret out.
- **Line-up**: the secret plus 11 others from the theme, shuffled. Tap a card to read it, cross it out or
  pick it. The definitions mean you can play a thing you have never heard of.
- **Score**: 1000 for a pick in up to 3 questions, minus 75 for each question after that, at least 200 for
  a right pick; 0 for a wrong one. Asking everything is safe but slow; stopping early is a gamble.

## Modes

- **Daily**: themes take turns (one a day), and each theme goes through its 16 things in a fixed shuffled
  order, so 224 days pass before a thing comes back. One try, resumes after a reload, settled once if lost.
- **Practice**: any theme or a chosen one, endless.
- **Friends' challenge**: the same round from the challenge seed.

## Share

`📦 Whatsit #3 · 🎻 · 4 questions · ✅` and the answers as 🟩/🟥 (spoiler-free without the questions).

## Original

Twenty Questions and Guess Who are old parlour-game ideas; Whatsit's themes, questions, definitions,
parcel character and art are its own. English and Bulgarian (`contentLangs`), following the site language.
