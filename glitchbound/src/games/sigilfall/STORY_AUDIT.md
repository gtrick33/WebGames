# Sigilfall — story audit (2026-11-14)

The owner asked for every quest to be checked for dialogue that contradicts itself. Every quest, reward, NPC line,
fight bark, scene line and vision caption was read, act by act, against the rest of the story and STORY_BIBLE.md.
About 105 discrepancies were found. The clear ones are fixed; four need the owner's call.

## Fixed (highlights)

- **Counts and numbers:** the vault at the Gloam Gate holds five sigils, not six. Kill counts in text now match the
  objectives (Emberdeep's six forge-slaves, Hollowmere's six drowned, Brin's six marsh-lights). The camp counts the
  villages that actually come (Fernstead, Millbrook, Hearthstead), not the retired lands' healers and smiths.
- **People in two places:** Mara leaves Briarhollow while she commands Highwall and the Last March. Elyra leaves the
  camp when she goes south with the hero (Act III), and leaves Lastlight for the Reach (Act VI). Nyx moves from
  Highwall to Lastlight when the lamp road starts. Orren leaves each land when he moves on.
- **Lines that outlive their moment:** the camp stops talking as if Elyra were lost once she is freed. Varn and the
  Regent wait for Highwall to hold before saying so. Hollis and Mother Alsa have lines for their time in the camp.
  Several later lines were hidden behind earlier ones (talk takes the first match) and now come first.
- **Who did what:** you tell Mara, not the Regent, that Highwall holds. Aurel let the plague ship in against the
  quarantine (the Sun vision), everywhere. Silvaine no longer says she asked Old Root for her brother's death.
  Nyx goes up Gallows Hill instead of "gone". The Hangman isn't dead before his own quest.
- **Time:** Sir Corvane is the Ninefold Watch knight who fell ten years ago (not a 300-year-old warden). The Sun
  King's tomb and its keeper are three hundred years old, not three thousand. Ashkar was chained a month.
- **Things never given:** no seals at Hollowmere, no flames at Lantern Hill, armor "found on the roads" in the
  Weeping Woods, obsidian found at Emberdeep's smelters, the ward-stone in Hroki's keeping.
- **Order of quests:** the Highwall sortie waits for Hollowbane (its text starts with his trench broken). Thessa
  hears that Elyra is gone once.
- **Names:** Grimtusk is a boar. Haldor's grandson is Robin (Pip Tanner is someone else). Hale's sister is Ada
  (Brin is Lastlight's lamp-keeper), and Marit is Hale's niece, as the Bible says.

## Open: the owner's call

1. **The night Vaelmoor died.** The Weeping Woods, Ysane's echo and Vaelor's last words say the King rode north to
   fetch a fallen star for the fever and came back too late. The Tides, Thorns and Glimmerwood visions (and the
   Bible) say he sat up all night between their beds and broke his crown when they died. The crier in the Crown's
   memory also announces a star falling in Vaelor's time, while the first vision dates the star to a thousand years
   earlier. One version has to go; the Bible's (the bedside) is the one the visions already tell.
2. **Elyra in Act V.** She now stays at Lastlight until the Last March, so "I could feel it from here" and "Orren
   wrote" hold, and Hollowmere's reward has you bring her the Tides. If she should travel with the hero in Act V
   instead, those lines change.
3. **Halvard's side.** He is called a warden of Aster in the Weeping Woods, but "held this hall against his own king"
   in Duskspire.
4. **Retired lands** (Greyfen, Saltmarsh, Ironpeak, Duskfen, Sablewind, Stormwatch, Bonefields, Blightwood, Vinecoil,
   Grimward, the Sunken Reaches) still contradict the live story in places (where the Tides and Stone sigils were,
   who opened the harbor). Nobody plays them; they would need the same pass if they come back.
