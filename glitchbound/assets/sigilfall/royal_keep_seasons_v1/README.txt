ROYAL KEEP - SEASONS  v1.1
Autumn and winter repaints of the Royal Keep tileset (autumn, winter).

Each folder is a drop-in copy of three folders from the main pack:

    tileset/    the atlas, the baked atlas and the terrain sheets
    props/      every prop, loose and on the group sheets
    animated/   the animated props (strips, frames, GIFs)
    preview/    two sample maps in this season

File names, sizes, tile ids and sheet positions are the same as in the main
pack, so the Tiled .tsx files and the Godot .tres files keep working: copy a
season's folders over the main ones, or point the tileset image at the season's
atlas.

What changes: grass, trees, bushes, ivy, dirt and (in winter) water. Stone, wood,
roofs, market goods and characters are the same in every season.
