# Playtest kit: watch 5 people play

Analytics tell you *where* players leave. Watching people tells you *why*. Five sessions of about
20 minutes each find most of the problems a site like GlitchBound has.

## Who

Five people who have never seen the site, ideally mixed:

- two who rarely play games (a parent, a grandparent),
- one teenager,
- one puzzle or Wordle fan,
- one Bulgarian speaker who plays in Bulgarian (to check the Bulgarian Wait, What?, Crass Act and the site text).

Friends are fine, as long as you don't help them.

## Setup

- Their own phone if possible (that's how most people will play), otherwise a laptop.
- Send them the plain link: https://glitchboundstudio.com. Don't explain anything.
- Sit next to them (or share screens on a call) and take notes. Ask them to **think out loud**.
- The golden rule: **don't help**. If they ask "what do I do?", say "what would you try?". Only step in
  after a minute of being stuck, and write down that you did.

## The tasks (read each one out, one at a time)

1. "Have a look around and tell me what this site is." (stop after 30 seconds: what did they understand?)
2. "Find a game you'd like to play and play it." (let them pick)
3. "Play today's Crosswire." (the most unusual rules: does the walkthrough do its job?)
4. "Play today's Wing It." (reflex game with three tries a day)
5. "Imagine friends are coming over tonight. Find something to play together." (Party Mode)
6. "Show me how you'd send your result to a friend."

## What to watch for

Write down the moment and what happened:

- **First 10 seconds of each game**: do they know what to do without reading?
- **Hesitations**: pauses longer than ~3 seconds, hovering, scrolling up and down.
- **Wrong taps**: tapping things that aren't buttons, missing things that are.
- **Words they say**: "huh?", "wait", "oh!", "I don't get it", laughs. Quote them exactly.
- **Where they'd stop**: ask "would you keep playing now, or close this?" after each game.
- **Phone problems**: anything cut off, too small, or hidden behind the keyboard.

## After: three questions

1. "What was the most fun moment?"
2. "What was the most annoying moment?"
3. "Would you come back tomorrow? Why / why not?"

## Afterwards

- Anything that tripped up **two or more** people is a real problem: fix it first.
- Anything only one person hit: note it, and watch for it in the next round.
- Check Admin › Stats for the same week: do the drop-offs you saw show up in the numbers too?
- Run another round of five after the fixes. It's the cheapest quality boost the site can get.
