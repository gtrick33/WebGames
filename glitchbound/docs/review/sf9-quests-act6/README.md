# Acts IV, V and VI quests against the Prologue's standard (piece 4 of NINE_PLAN.md, 2026-11-25)

Built on the Act III branch (the save table's end is shared). Acts IV to VI are Highwall, the Gloamhollow, the
Weeping Woods, Emberdeep, Hollowmere and the Pale Reach with Crownfall: 80 quests in these lands' files, 33 of them
with no step but going somewhere or talking to someone.

## Acts IV and V: read, and left as they are

Every walk-or-talk quest in them is the road into a land, a search that finds something kept and sets up the next
fight, or a scene the story turns on (Nyx's confession, Ysane's last echo). Their side stories already turn:

| Land | Side story | Its turn, already there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Highwall | the names of the fallen | the Knights' Chapel and what is found in it |
| Gloamhollow | the Black Pool | what the pool keeps, then the Drowned Mother |
| Weeping Woods | the Hanging Oaks; the Wolf Barrow | letters never sent; a hound guarding a grave his master never came home to fill |
| Emberdeep | the Drake Roost | what is found at the roost |
| Hollowmere | a pilgrim's errand; Digger Mott's dead | the keepsakes of Elsmere; the guards on the road are the people Mott buried |

Emberdeep's brewer (two quests about her pipes) is a plain errand and was left one: the land has its story, and not
every errand needs a secret. So there is no pull request for Act IV or Act V.

## Act VI: one side story turns, and closes Act II's question

**Quartermaster Holt's plate** (`cold-iron`, then `riveted-coats`) was armor taken from pale knights and riveted to
the army's coats, ending in a pair of greaves. It now ends with *A Plate with Aster's Armory Stamp*: under the black
the plates carry Aster's own armory mark, ten years old, mustered for "the relief of the Ninefold Watch". In Act II
the Regent says her treasury paid for that relief, that it never marched, and that "that money went somewhere"
(`city-the-treasury`). This is where its steel went. Holt rivets it on anyway and sends the stamped plate south.
A keepsake in the Journal, a new ending, and a new line from Holt afterwards.

The Pale Reach's other side stories (Wintersend, the hound named Bran) already turn on what they find. The finale's
quests were not touched.
