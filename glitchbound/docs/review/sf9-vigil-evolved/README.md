# The Vigil's evolutions, bolder (2026-11-26)

`strips/<class>.jpg`: for each of the class's spells that evolve, the spell on one row and its evolution under it, at
the same moments after each is set going (0.18 s, 0.42 s, 0.7 s, 1.05 s), at a phone's size on its side (844×390,
cropped round the hero), with this Mac's GPU. A still undersells what moves; four frames show the form, the landing
and what it leaves on the ground.

Made by `node scripts/sigilfall-vigil-spellsheet.mjs --out docs/review/sf9-vigil-evolved/strips --strip --gpu`. Each
spell is alone at its top level in a quiet night, with a ring of the Host walking in slowly; a dash spell is set off by
a dash, a pulse with the hero hurt, and a ward is broken by a blow at the third frame.

What the crop leaves out: a lance is 230 across, longer than the picture is tall, so Sentence's sword (which falls at
the far end of its line) and the far half of Glacierfall's and Noonday's lines are outside it.
