# GlitchBound brand kit

The marketing material uses the same look as the site: a bright toy box. Warm paper, ink outlines,
hard offset shadows and saturated accents. Everything in this folder follows these rules, so new
pieces (made in Canva, CapCut or the studio) should too.

## Logo

- **The mark** (`logos/mark/`): a bold white G in a blue rounded square, a coral/teal misprint offset
  (the "glitch") and lime bounding-box corners. Use the PNGs as they are; never redraw, recolor,
  stretch or rotate the mark, and don't remove the offset.
- **Lockups** (`logos/lockup/`): horizontal (mark + wordmark) and stacked (with the motto). Use the
  `-dark` versions on dark or busy backgrounds, the `-light` ones on paper or white.
- **Wordmark** (`logos/wordmark/`): "Glitch" in ink, "Bound" in cyan (#0a74a0 on light, #22e4ff on dark).
- **Profile pictures** (`logos/profile-pictures/`): `avatar-blue.png` is the default for TikTok,
  Instagram, YouTube, X and Discord (all crop to a circle; the G and corners sit inside it).
- **Watermark** (`logos/watermark/`): for a corner of your own edits.
- Clear space: at least half the mark's width on every side. Minimum size: 48 px tall on screen.

## Type

- **Bricolage Grotesque** (800 weight, tight tracking -0.045em) for headlines, 600-700 for body.
- **Space Mono** (bold, uppercase, wide tracking) for small labels and chips.
- Both are open-source (SIL OFL, files and licences in `_studio/kit/fonts/`).
- Highlight one or two key words per line with a yellow marker block. Never more than three per post.

## Shapes

- Cards: white, 6 px ink outline (at 1080 px wide), 40-54 px corner radius, an 18 px hard ink shadow
  down-right. No blur shadows.
- Stickers and chips: pill shapes with the same outline and a smaller hard shadow, slightly rotated.
- Confetti: circles, triangles, stars and squiggles in the accent colors, ink-outlined.

## Voice

- Short, playful, confident. "Small games. Big obsessions." "No download. No sign-up. Just play."
- Honest: only claim what the games really do. No invented stats ("only 2% can..."), no fake
  urgency. Scores in the videos are real runs.
- Inclusive and family-friendly by default. Crass Act (18+) stays out of general marketing.
- No emoji drawn into artwork or videos (captions are fine: the platform renders them).
- Motto: **Small games. Big obsessions.**
- URL: **glitchboundstudio.com** · handle suggestion: **@glitchboundstudio**

## Platform safe zones (vertical video, 1080x1920)

Keep text and faces out of: the top 220 px (status bar, tabs), the bottom 420 px (captions, music
row) and the right 150 px (like / comment / share buttons). The studio's templates already do.
